First Engagement

Crime and public safety data systems

We are scoping our first engagement with a Nigerian security institution. The goal: build simple data tools that get crime evidence from the officer on the ground to the commander making patrol decisions.

Built with frontline officers. Using familiar tools. Run by internal champions after we leave.
