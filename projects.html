Our Work

Projects.

One engagement at a time. Year 1 focus: building a proof of concept with the Nigerian Police Force that demonstrates what data tools can do for public institutions.

Active · Year 1 · 2026–2027

Nigerian Police Force
Crime Data Systems

Nigerian police divisions track crime incidents manually in logbooks or disconnected Excel files. No real-time visibility of crime patterns across divisions. Commanders lack data to make patrol deployment decisions.

We are building and piloting three simple data tools with the Police Force — co-designed with frontline officers — that will allow commanders to make data-driven decisions daily.

Crime Incident Dashboard (Power BI + Google Sheets)

WhatsApp Patrol Route Tracker

Citizen Feedback System (QR Code + Google Forms)

Phase

Diagnosis (Jul–Sep 2026)

Pilot Size

1–2 Divisions, 30 Officers

Completion

March 2027

Adoption Target

70% of pilot users

Project Timeline

Jul–Sep 2026 · Phase 1: Diagnose

MoU signing, needs assessment, officer interviews, data audit

Oct–Dec 2026 · Phase 2: Build

Co-ideation workshops, tool development, user testing

Jan–Mar 2027 · Phase 3: Pilot & Hand Over

Pilot rollout, training, impact assessment, case study publication

Success Metrics

70% of officers logging incidents within 24 hours

Commanders checking dashboard at least weekly

At least 1 operational decision informed by dashboard data

Officers report "system saves 2 hours/week on paperwork"
