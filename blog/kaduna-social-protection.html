A data pilot in Kaduna, starting with social protection

November 2026  ·  Abiola Oyebanjo

We are putting our approach to work in one place first. A proof of concept is under way in Kaduna State, applying it to social protection.

Social protection only works when support reaches the people who qualify. In practice it often does not. The agency that knows who a person is, the one that decides whether they qualify, and the one that delivers the benefit are usually separate, and they cannot easily check each other's records. People fall through the gaps, some are served twice, and budgets leak.

Our work in Kaduna State is aimed squarely at that problem. The goal is simple. Help the agencies that run support programmes confirm who qualifies, cut duplication, and reach more of the right people. The method is the one we use everywhere. Make each agency's own data usable first, then let agencies answer each other's questions without moving anyone's records between offices.

No central database. No agency hands over its files. Only a verified answer travels.

This work is being developed with government partners in Kaduna State. We have signed our first formal agreement, and further state agencies are in discussion.

It is early, and we are deliberately not promising outcomes we have not yet measured. What we can say is that the whole approach now runs end to end, with no records moved, and the first agency relationships are in place. We will share more as the work develops.
