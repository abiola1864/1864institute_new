The Problem We Solve

Nigerian government agencies collect data they cannot use.

01

Crime, Violence & Peace

Officers log crimes in notebooks. Commanders guess where to send patrols. The data that could guide better decisions never reaches the people making them.

Why this is our focus →
02

Gender

Institutions collect service data but never disaggregate it by gender. Disparities are invisible in the aggregate. What institutions cannot see, they cannot fix.

How We Work Differently

Evidence. Simple tech.
Co-creation.

Not strategy documents. Not expensive software. Working tools, built with the people who use them, that survive the end of the project.

01

Evidence

We listen to frontline workers before designing anything. Evidence of the real problem shapes everything that follows. We measure honestly and publish what we learn.

02

Simple Technology

No new software. No IT department required. We build with tools government workers already have — systems that run without us when we leave.

03

Co-Creation

Frontline workers shape the tools from day one. When workers own what they helped build, they use it. That is why our tools outlast our presence.

Our Focus · 2026–2027

Two areas. Two years of focused work.

01

Crime, Violence & Peace

How do security institutions use data to make better decisions?

We work with Nigerian security institutions to build simple tools that get crime evidence from the officer on the ground to the commander making patrol decisions — co-designed, sustainable, and measurable.

Why we chose this focus →
02

Gender

Where are the data gaps that make gender disparities invisible?

Simple additions to existing data collection can surface what was always there but never seen. Once institutions can see a disparity, they can begin to address it. We build the tools that make that possible.
