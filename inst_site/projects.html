Where we are

We are building deliberately, not quickly.

The 1864 Institute was registered in March 2026. We are currently in the organisational setup phase: finalising board agreements, developing our research methodology, and identifying the right institutional partner for our first engagement.

Our north star for Year 1 is one well-documented proof of concept, not multiple weak engagements. We would rather start late and do it right than rush into something that does not produce real evidence of impact.

What a project looks like

Each engagement follows our 9-month model: three months listening and scoping, three months co-designing and building with frontline workers, three months piloting and handing over. The output is a working tool, an impact report, and a published case study.

We work in the two areas where we have deep expertise and where data tools have the clearest potential: crime, violence and peace, and gender.
