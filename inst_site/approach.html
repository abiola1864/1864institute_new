Our Methodology

How we work.

A 9-month embedded partnership model built around co-design, familiar technology, and sustainable handover. We don't parachute in, build something, and leave.

The 9-Month Partnership Model

Three phases. One goal: a working tool that government staff run themselves.

1

Months 1–3

Listen

We begin by understanding the real problem — not the assumed one. This means getting out of meeting rooms and into the daily workflows of the people who will use the tools we build.

Interview frontline staff — not just leadership. Officers, teachers, health workers tell us what they actually need.

Shadow workers to see actual daily workflows. We watch how data is currently collected and what happens to it.

Audit current data systems: what exists, why it isn't used, what has been tried before and why it failed.

Research what has worked in comparable contexts across Nigeria and Africa.

Output: Scoping Report

"Here's the real problem and what will actually work" — written for the institution, not for a funder.

2

Months 4–7

Build Together

We build with users, not for users. Co-design workshops bring 8–10 frontline workers into the room to shape exactly what gets built. We prototype fast, test with real users, and improve based on feedback.

Co-design workshops: officers/teachers/workers tell us what they need, we prototype, they test it, we improve.

Use tools they already know: Excel, Power BI, WhatsApp, Google Sheets. Not expensive software they can't maintain.

Create simple training materials — screenshots and walkthroughs, not 50-page technical manuals.

Output: Working Tool + User Guide

A functioning prototype that real users have tested and shaped — not a mockup or a demo.

3

Months 7–9

Pilot & Hand Over

We pilot in 1–2 real locations with 20–30 users, provide hands-on training, give weekly support during the first month, and train internal champions who sustain the system after we leave. When the project ends, the system doesn't die.

Pilot in 1–2 locations with real users. Monitor weekly: are people using it? What's improving?

Train internal champions — government staff who support their colleagues and keep the system running.

Impact assessment with real data: we track what works and what doesn't, and publish lessons learned openly.

Output: Impact Report + Published Case Study + Sustainability Plan

A documented, replicable model other institutions can learn from.

Our Principles

What sets us apart

📊

Evidence-Based

We let data guide decisions — ours and our partners'. We track what works and what doesn't, and we publish both honestly.

🔧

Practical

We build what works, not what sounds good. Working tools. Not strategy documents. Not expensive software. Things frontline workers actually use.

🤝

Collaborative

We co-create with government partners — not for them. The people who use the tools shape the tools. That's why adoption rates are high.

📖

Transparent

We share our learnings openly — what worked and what didn't. Our case studies are published. Our methodology is documented. Our IP is our reputation.

🇳🇬

Contextual

We design for Nigerian realities — intermittent power, limited bandwidth, familiar tools. Not solutions imported from elsewhere and forced to fit.

⚡

Lean & Adaptive

Small team, fast decisions, direct access to decision-makers. We respond to what we learn rather than following a rigid plan that ignores reality.
