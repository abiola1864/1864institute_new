How We Work

Our approach.

We embed inside government institutions, co-design data tools with the people who will use them, and hand everything over so the work continues after we leave.

A 9-month embedded partnership

Three phases. One principle: we build with government workers, not for them.

Months 1 to 3

Listen

We interview frontline staff, observe daily workflows, and audit what data systems already exist. We do not assume we know the problem before we arrive.

Months 4 to 7

Build Together

Co-design workshops with frontline users. Rapid prototyping using tools they already know. Test, get feedback, improve. Simple training materials that do not require an IT department.

Months 7 to 9

Hand Over

Pilot in real locations. Hands-on training. Impact assessment. Internal champions trained to sustain the tool. When we leave, the system does not die.

Years 1 and 2

Two focus areas.
Two years of focused work.

We are a young institute and we are deliberate about scope. For 2026 and 2027, all of our work sits within two areas where the evidence gaps are large and the potential for data tools to change outcomes is real.

Better to produce one rigorous, well-documented proof of concept than to spread thin across many sectors. Our north star in Year 1 is a single institutional engagement that creates a replicable model.

Crime, Violence & Peace

How do Nigerian security institutions use data to respond to crime? What do frontline officers need to make better decisions? How can simple tools improve public safety outcomes?

Gender

How do gender dynamics shape access to public services and economic opportunity in Nigeria? Where are the data gaps that prevent institutions from seeing and responding to gender-based disparities?
