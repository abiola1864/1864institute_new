The Board

Our team.

Three board members. Clear roles. No overhead. Research rigour + M&E expertise + institutional access — the exact combination needed to make government technology actually work.

Dr. Abiola Oyebanjo

Executive Director

abiola@1864institute.org

Focus Area

Strategy, Institutional Access, Fundraising

Year 1 Deliverable

Police Force MoU + Website + Brand

Abiola Oyebanjo is a sociologist and researcher with deep expertise in experimental research design, institutional analysis, and evidence-based policy in Nigeria. He holds a PhD in Sociology (Magna Cum Laude) from Humboldt University Berlin and is a co-author of a Nature publication on research reproducibility.

He has spent years embedded inside Nigerian regulatory institutions — including the Central Bank of Nigeria, the Nigerian Communications Commission, and the Kaduna State Investment Promotion Agency — helping them build data systems and analytical capacity.

At the 1864 Institute, Abiola leads institutional engagement, fundraising, and external communications. He is the primary point of contact for government partnerships.

Dr. Nneka Osadolor

Director of Research

Focus Area

Research Quality, Methodology, Publications

Year 1 Deliverable

1–2 Policy Briefs + Methodology Library

Dr. Nneka Osadolor is a researcher whose work focuses on gender, institutions, and development in the Nigerian context. She brings academic rigour and a commitment to methodological integrity that underpins all of the 1864 Institute's outputs.

At the 1864 Institute, Nneka reviews all scoping reports and deliverables, oversees the methodology library, leads the publication strategy, and designs capacity building modules for partner institutions.

She is the research quality guarantee — ensuring that what the Institute produces meets the standards required to be taken seriously by academic and policy audiences alike.

Akintoye Oyekunle

Director of Operations & M&E

Focus Area

M&E, Operations, Budget, Staff

Year 1 Deliverable

M&E Framework + POC Implementation

Akintoye Oyekunle Oluwatobi brings expertise in M&E framework design, operational systems, and results measurement to the 1864 Institute. He is responsible for the day-to-day running of the organisation and the integrity of its impact measurement.

In Year 1, Akintoye leads the design of the M&E framework for the Police Force engagement, manages the Research Associate, oversees budget compliance, and coordinates proof of concept implementation on the ground.

He also manages the Institute's Power BI dashboards for internal tracking — ensuring the organisation practises what it preaches when it comes to data-driven decision-making.
