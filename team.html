Dr. Abiola Oyebanjo

Executive Director

info@1864institute.org LinkedIn

Abiola has spent years building data systems and analytical tools inside Nigerian regulatory and public institutions, working directly with the staff who use them. He understands how public institutions collect data, where it gets stuck, and what it takes to make it useful for daily decisions.

At the 1864 Institute, he leads institutional engagement and partnerships. He is the primary contact for government institutions considering a data tool engagement.

Dr. Nneka Osadolor

Director of Research

LinkedIn

Nneka oversees research quality across all of the Institute's work. She ensures that the data tools we build are designed around sound evidence of the problem, and that the impact assessments we publish meet the standards required to influence policy.

She leads the Institute's documentation and publication strategy, and reviews all deliverables before they are shared with institutional partners.

Akintoye Oyekunle

Director of Operations

LinkedIn

Akintoye manages the Institute's operations and oversees impact measurement. He designs the monitoring and evaluation frameworks that track whether the tools we build are actually being used and what they are changing on the ground.

He manages project timelines, partner coordination, and the day-to-day running of engagements. ensuring the tools we build get properly piloted, measured, and handed over.
